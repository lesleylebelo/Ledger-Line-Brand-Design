Solutions

One system, shaped around your part of the trade.

Ledgerline works the same way underneath for every business — the difference is which parts of it matter most to you.

Wholesale

Bulk pricing, minimum order quantities and customer-specific price lists — set up once in Ledgerline and applied automatically at the point of sale.

  • Tiered and customer-specific pricing without spreadsheet lookups
  • Bulk order entry built for speed, not retail-style checkouts
  • Live stock visibility so you never sell what you don't have

Distribution

Multi-branch stock, route-ready order batching, and a single view of inventory across every warehouse you run.

  • Real-time stock across every branch and vehicle
  • Batch and route orders for efficient delivery days
  • Automatic reordering thresholds by location

Merchants

Flexible catalogue management for businesses selling across multiple channels, with pricing and stock that stay in sync everywhere.

  • One catalogue, multiple sales channels
  • Channel-specific pricing without duplicate data entry
  • Consolidated reporting across every outlet

Retail

Point-of-sale-ready stock counts, simple staff workflows, and reporting that tells you what's actually selling.

  • Stock counts that stay accurate at the till, not just in the back office
  • A simple enough interface for seasonal and part-time staff
  • Plain-language sales reporting, not raw data exports
