Creative Brief

"Every Line Adds Up" — Ledgerline autumn campaign

ClientLedgerline (fictional)
Prepared byDesign
StatusFor creative development

Background

Ledgerline provides ERP and trading-management software to wholesalers, distributors, merchants and retailers. Most of its target customers currently run stock, ordering and pricing across a patchwork of spreadsheets and disconnected legacy tools. Ledgerline's product update this quarter brings these into one connected system, and the brand needs a campaign to mark the moment.

Business problem

Buyers in this category have been burned before: ERP software has a reputation for being slow to implement, hard to use, and indistinguishable from every other vendor's pitch. Ledgerline needs to look and sound like the exception — modern and trustworthy without resorting to generic tech-startup visual language.

Target audience

Primary — the buyer: Operations Directors and owners at mid-size wholesale/distribution businesses, 35–55. Time-poor, skeptical of vendor pitches, motivated by reliability and reduced manual error over flashy features.

Secondary — the user: warehouse, sales and ops staff who'll use the system daily. Care about speed and simplicity far more than the buyer does.

Objectives

  • Reposition Ledgerline from "another ERP system" to the connective layer that makes a distribution business legible to itself
  • Build a recognisable, ownable visual and verbal system that can flex across print, digital and campaign use
  • Drive qualified demo requests from the primary buyer audience

Key message

Every line adds up.

Tone of voice

Direct, competent, human. Short sentences. No exclamation marks, no jargon, no false urgency. Confidence comes from clarity, not volume — this is a brand that never needs to shout to be taken seriously.

ReliableDirectModernHuman

Deliverables

  • Campaign concept, headline and supporting copy
  • LinkedIn / social graphic
  • Digital advertising units (MPU + leaderboard)
  • Email marketing header graphic
  • Website promotional banner
  • Standalone promotional/share graphic
  • Sales presentation cover slide
  • A4 brochure / one-page leave-behind

Design requirements

  • Full adherence to the Ledgerline brand guidelines (colour, type, logo usage)
  • Every asset must work standalone — no asset should depend on another to make sense
  • Amber accent used once per asset maximum, reserved for the single most important element
  • All assets must be legible at their real-world viewing size (a 300×250 ad viewed at actual pixel size, not zoomed in)

Success criteria

Because this is a self-initiated, fictional exercise, success is measured by design quality and consistency, not real-world performance data. The campaign succeeds if: the visual system holds together across every format, the headline and message stay legible and consistent throughout, and someone unfamiliar with the brief could look at any single asset and correctly identify it as Ledgerline.
