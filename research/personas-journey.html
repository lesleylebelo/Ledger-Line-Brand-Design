UX Research

Personas & primary user journey

Personas

Economic buyer

Priya Nathan

"I don't need more features. I need to trust the number on my screen."
Role
Operations Director, mid-size wholesale distributor (40 staff)
Goals
Reduce manual reconciliation, get a reliable stock and pricing picture across branches, avoid a painful implementation
Frustrations
Every vendor demo looks the same; past ERP rollout took six months and staff never fully adopted it
What earns her attention
Plain language, a fast path to a real demo, evidence the product understands trade businesses specifically
Daily user

Tom Ridley

"If it slows me down on the warehouse floor, I'll go back to the spreadsheet."
Role
Warehouse & Ops Supervisor
Goals
Update stock and process orders quickly, with minimal training, without the system getting in his way
Frustrations
Cluttered legacy interfaces, features he doesn't need, systems that assume desk-based, not floor-based, work
What earns his attention
Speed and clarity, not marketing — he'll never see the homepage, but every decision made for Priya has to hold up for him too

Primary journey — from cold visit to demo request

Mapped for Priya, the economic buyer, since she's the one who initiates contact. Each stage maps directly to a homepage section — see the information architecture for the structural version of this journey.

01 — Arrive

Lands on homepage

From a search, a LinkedIn ad, or a colleague's referral. Skeptical, has seen a dozen ERP sites before.

Hero + product intro
02 — Evaluate

Scans for relevance

Checks whether this is built for a business like hers, not generic enterprise software.

Industries served + benefits
03 — Build trust

Looks for proof

Wants evidence this actually works day-to-day, not just marketing claims.

Capability band + testimonial
04 — Act

Requests a demo

Ready to commit 20 minutes, not a sales call — the CTA has to promise something specific and low-risk.

CTA band + demo form
